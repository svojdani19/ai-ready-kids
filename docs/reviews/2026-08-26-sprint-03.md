# Sprint 03 review — 26 August 2026

**Scope.** Completion-state integrity. Sprint 02 made every *answer* wait for
the server; independent review found the principle stopped one step short of
the end in both child flows, at the two moments that decide whether a piece of
work counts at all.

**Method.** Ran against the checklist in `../classroom-review.md`. Both student
flows driven at 768×1024 with `window.fetch` replaced by a rejecting stub at
the exact moment of completion, then restored and retried, with the database
inspected at each step. Classroom Mode regression-checked at 1280×800.

---

## Findings and what changed

### 1. The badge was claimed before it was recorded — fixed

Reaching the ending scene rendered *Badge earned*, the earned sticker, and live
*Back to my missions* / *Take-home page* links, all while `finishMission` was
still in flight. On slow school wifi a child could read the congratulations and
leave before anything was written — landing back on a map that still said the
mission was unfinished. On failure the retry appeared but the exit links stayed
active, so the escape route out of the problem was also the route that lost the
work.

**Completion now has an explicit save state.** While pending, the panel reads
*Saving your badge…* with the sticker unearned and the only control a disabled
*Saving…*. Once recorded, the panel turns and says *Badge earned* and the exits
appear. On failure the ending and the full wrap-up stay on screen, the panel
says *Not saved yet — Your badge is not lost*, and the single action is
**Try again**. The mission is never described as earned until it is persisted.

The mid-mission *Save and exit* link is also hidden on the ending scene: a
second, ungated way out would have defeated the gate. Children are not trapped
— the persistent student header (Map, My badges, Log out) is always available,
so the gate closes the *completion* actions without closing the room.

### 2. Check-in finalisation had no failure handling at all — fixed

`finish()` awaited `finishCheckIn` and pushed to `/student` with no `catch`. A
rejection left the child on a dead screen with a spinner that never resolved,
and produced the worst possible record: nine saved answers with no completion
marker, which every report and every growth calculation then treats as an
unfinished check-in.

Finalisation now waits visibly (*Saving…*, button disabled, extra taps
ignored), catches failure, and keeps the child on the closing screen with
*"All 9 of your answers are safe. Tap Try again when you are ready."* The route
to `/student` happens only after the marker succeeds. Retries are idempotent:
the server stamps a completion time only if there is not one already, so no
number of retries can move it or duplicate it.

### 3. A child was told they had "already finished" as they finished — fixed

Found during the browser pass on the success path. Recording completion
re-renders the route, which flips `alreadyCompleted` to true, which made the
replay note appear in the same breath as the first completion: *"You already
finished this one."* The note now uses the value captured when the player
mounted, so it means what it says.

---

## Checked and found acceptable

- **Replays stay immediate and write-free.** A finished mission starts in the
  saved state, calls nothing, shows the badge at once and opens its exits
  immediately. Asserted by test.
- **Nothing partial is written.** With the network down at the ending, the
  database held all four decisions and `completed_at` null; the check-in held
  all nine answers and `completed_at` null. After one retry each: exactly one
  completion timestamp, one row, no duplicates.
- **Feedback still does not wait.** Authored feedback and the wrap-up are local
  content and appear instantly. Only the claims about records wait on records.
- **Copy is honest and calm under failure.** *Not saved yet*, *Your badge is
  not lost*, *All 9 of your answers are safe*. No blame, no alarm, and no
  suggestion that the child did something wrong.
- **Accessibility.** The completion panel is a `polite` live region so the
  change from saving to earned is announced; failures are `role="alert"`; every
  state has a real label rather than a disabled unlabelled control.
- **Classroom Mode regression.** Reveal, touch switching A → C without leaving
  the decision, *Show the choices*, hands-up tally and keyboard routes all
  still correct at 1280×800.

---

## Deliberately left

- **No automatic retry.** Still one explicit, child-driven retry. A silent
  background retry is exactly the invisible behaviour these three sprints have
  been removing.
- **No local draft of completion.** Tempting to record "finished" in the
  browser and reconcile later, but a completion marker that exists only on one
  Chromebook is a worse lie than an honest failure, and it would let a badge
  appear on a device while the teacher's dashboard disagrees.
- **The disabled "Saving…" control has no timeout.** If a request hangs
  forever the child sits on a saving state. The header nav is still available,
  and a spinner that gives up after N seconds would need a story about what it
  then claims. Worth revisiting with a real network policy in production.
