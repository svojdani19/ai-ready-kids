# Sprint 04 review — 26 August 2026

**Scope.** Two findings from independent review: a returning child being marched
back through a check-in they had already answered, and an invisible keyboard
focus indicator on the check-in options.

**Method.** Ran against the checklist in `../classroom-review.md`. Both flows
driven at 768×1024 with real keyboard events, computed styles read from the
live DOM rather than inferred, and the database compared byte-for-byte before
and after. Focus checked again at an emulated 200% zoom (384×512). Classroom
Mode regression at 1280×800.

---

## Findings and what changed

### 1. A fully answered but unfinalized check-in restarted from story one — fixed

Sprint 03 made a failed finalisation safe: nine answers stay on the server with
no completion marker. What it did not consider was the child coming *back*.

`CheckInPlayer` computed its starting index as `firstUnanswered === -1 ? 0`,
folding "everything is already answered" into "start at the beginning". A child
who closed the tab after a failed finish reopened the check-in at the intro and
was walked through all nine stories again — every one of them already saved, and
every one of them now re-answerable. The measurement would survive, but only
because the child was being asked to redo work that was never lost, and any
answer they changed on the second pass would silently overwrite a first-pass
answer that was the real one.

**"All authored items answered, completion marker missing" is now its own
state.** The player opens straight on a finishing screen headed *Let's finish
saving*, which says *"You already answered all 9 stories, and every one of them
is safe. There is nothing to do again."* and offers a single **Finish**. No
stories are rendered, no radios exist, and `submitCheckInAnswer` is never
called — asserted by test and confirmed against the database, where the stored
responses came back byte-identical after finishing.

A partly answered check-in is untouched: it still goes through the intro and
resumes at the first story without an answer. An empty one is still a fresh
start. Only the exact recovery case is special-cased.

### 2. Tabbing into an unanswered option group showed no focus at all — fixed

The real `<input type="radio">` is `sr-only`, so the global focus ring was being
painted on a clipped one-pixel box. A keyboard or switch user tabbing into a
group they had not yet answered saw nothing move. The selected state was
visible; the *focused* state was not, which is the one that tells you where you
are.

The ring now lives on the card the child can actually see, driven by the hidden
radio's own focus:

- `has-[input:focus-visible]:outline-4` with a 3px offset in marigold-deep,
  sitting outside the card border.
- `focus-visible:outline-none` on the input itself, so the clipped element
  stops drawing its own.

Marigold was chosen because the selected state is denim: a card that is both
focused and chosen shows a denim border and wash *inside* a marigold ring, and
the two remain separately legible. Confirmed in the browser, not assumed —
computed style on the focused label reads `solid 4px rgb(156, 86, 5)` with the
input at `outline-style: none`, and the generated stylesheet carries the
`:has(:is(input:focus-visible))` rule with `--tw-outline-style` defaulting to
`solid`.

At an emulated 200% zoom the ring is unchanged, the card reflows to three lines
without horizontal overflow (`scrollWidth === innerWidth`), and both states stay
distinguishable.

## Checked and found acceptable

- **Keyboard flow.** Tab reaches the group, arrow keys move and select within it
  without submitting anything, and the roving tab stop lands on the chosen
  option when returning to an answered group. Confirmed with real key events.
- **Nothing rewritten in recovery.** Stored responses identical before and after
  finishing; one row; one completion timestamp.
- **Sprint 03 behaviour intact.** Finalisation still waits visibly, still keeps
  every answer on failure, still routes only on success, still idempotent.
- **Classroom Mode regression at 1280×800.** Reveal, touch switching A → C
  without leaving the decision, *Show the choices*, hands-up tally and keyboard
  routes all correct.
- **No new data, and no chatbot.** This sprint added no field, no table and no
  network call. Nothing in the product accepts free text from a child, and
  nothing generates text at runtime; both remain true.
- **Copy.** The recovery screen tells a seven year old the truth in their own
  terms — the work is safe, there is nothing to redo, one tap finishes it.

---

## Deliberately left

- **No way to review answers from the recovery screen.** Considered and
  rejected. There is nothing a child could usefully do with a review at that
  point except change an answer, which is exactly what a recovery must not
  invite. A teacher who needs to reopen a check-in can delete and reassign.
- **Focus ring colour is fixed rather than themed.** Marigold works against both
  the paper and denim-wash backgrounds the card can have. A school accent
  colour could collide with it; if branding ever reaches student surfaces, this
  ring needs to be re-derived rather than inherited.
- **200% zoom checked by viewport emulation, not real browser zoom.** Layout
  reflow is faithful; text rasterisation is not. Real zoom belongs in the
  assistive-technology audit that is still outstanding.
