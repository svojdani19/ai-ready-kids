# Sprint 65 — the term notices described a freeze the term gate never applied

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Reviewed against:** HEAD `cf0d312`
**Scope:** subscription notice copy only. No billing feature, no
account-management feature, no change to what the gate blocks.

## The defect

The subscription notices described the term state as if it froze the school's
records. The lapsed staff notice ended:

> …Nothing has been deleted and nothing is hidden. Everything the school already
> has stays here and stays readable.

That is a claim about the **whole store, for the whole duration of the lapse**,
and nothing in the product guarantees it. The term gate covers instructional
and classroom writes. It does not cover retention:

- `runScheduledPurge` (`src/lib/domain/purge.ts`) performs **no subscription
  check**. A cohort past its deletion date is deleted by the next purge run
  whether the term is active, lapsed or unreadable.
- `deleteClassDataAction` and `setRetentionAction` are on the gate's ALLOWED
  list, so an administrator can delete a class while the notice is on screen.

So a school could read *"nothing has been deleted"*, run `npm run purge` that
night as scheduled, and lose a cohort that was legitimately due. The sentence
was not merely imprecise — it contradicted a job the product ships and a button
the same administrator can press.

The Program & plan note carried the softer form of the same claim
(*"Nothing is deleted and nothing is hidden"*), and the admin overview had a
**second, drifted copy** of the unverified-term paragraph that had grown its own
version of the deletion claim.

## The correction

One shared constant, stating the **causal** fact instead of the global one:

```ts
const RETENTION_UNAFFECTED =
  "This does not itself delete or hide anything. Records still inside the school's " +
  "retention window remain available, along with reports and exports, and the retention " +
  "schedule the school configured and the administrator's own deletion controls carry on " +
  "as before.";
```

*Does not itself* is the whole correction: lapse is not a cause of deletion, and
the notice no longer promises that no other cause is running.

- `LAPSED_STAFF_BODY` and `UNVERIFIED_STAFF_BODY` both end in it. The
  needs-configuration distinctions survive intact — *"This is not an expiry —
  nothing has ended"* before it, and *"Ask your account contact to correct the
  subscription dates on the account"* after it. (Dropping that recovery
  instruction was my first attempt; an existing test caught it.)
- `src/app/admin/page.tsx` renders `UNVERIFIED_STAFF_TITLE` /
  `UNVERIFIED_STAFF_BODY` instead of its own paragraph.
- The Program & plan renewal note now says what lapse does *not* do and then
  what it explicitly does not suspend:

  > …Lapsing does not itself delete or hide anything, and your reports, exports,
  > retention settings and staff administration all keep working, because the
  > school owns its records whatever the invoice says. What it does not do is
  > suspend retention: the schedule you configured carries on, so a class past
  > its deletion date stays due, and it is deleted the next time your deployment
  > runs the purge job. Pausing neither brings that forward nor holds it back,
  > and nothing in this build runs the job on a timer, so due is not yet
  > deleted. …

- `LAPSED_WRITE_REFUSAL` keeps *"Nothing has been changed."* That one is
  transactionally true — the refused write is rejected before it touches the
  database — and it is a statement about the rejected action, not about the
  store.
- Child-facing copy (`LAPSED_STUDENT_MESSAGE`) is unchanged and still mentions
  no billing.

## What the tests pin

New block in `tests/subscription-lapse.test.ts` — *"a paused term does not pause
retention, and the copy says so"*, six cases:

- Two `it.each` **behaviour** tests over both paused states: `runScheduledPurge`
  still deletes a due cohort; retention dates and `deleteClass` still work while
  `assertClassSubscriptionActive` throws.
- Copy tests forbidding `nothing has been deleted`, `nothing is deleted and
  nothing is hidden`, `stays here and stays readable`, `everything the school
  already has`; asserting `does not itself delete or hide anything`, `still
  inside the school's retention window`, `retention schedule the school
  configured`, `deletion controls carry on`.
- One test keeping *"nothing has been changed"* confined to the refusal
  constants.
- One asserting the admin overview references the shared constant rather than
  duplicating its text.

**Honest note on the two behaviour tests: they pass before this sprint as well.**
They do not fail-before, because the product was already right — it was the copy
that was wrong. They are here so that a future change which *does* make lapse
freeze retention has to confront these assertions and the copy in the same
commit, rather than leaving the two to drift apart again.

The four tests that **do** fail on a stash of `subscription.ts`,
`admin/page.tsx` and `admin/program/page.tsx` are the copy assertions.

## Correction during acceptance — the replacement made its own timeline promise

My first version of that note ended *"so a class already past its deletion date
**is still deleted on time**."* That is a promise about a schedule this build
does not run. `admin/data/page.tsx` has always said so plainly:

> Nothing in this build runs it on a timer: a deployment schedules it, and until
> it runs, records past the date are still here. That is why this page says due
> rather than deleted.

So I replaced one unsupported claim with another, on the same subject, two pages
from the copy that contradicts it. **Due** and **deleted** are different facts,
and the note had collapsed them again — a missed or delayed cron leaves a cohort
present well past its date, and the note would have told an administrator it had
not.

The corrected wording keeps the two apart and names the actor:

> …so a class past its deletion date **stays due**, and it is deleted **the next
> time your deployment runs the purge job**. Pausing neither brings that forward
> nor holds it back, and nothing in this build runs the job on a timer, so due
> is not yet deleted.

The shared subscription constants needed no change: they speak of the schedule
*carrying on*, never of when a deletion happens. Retention behaviour is
untouched.

`tests/subscription-lapse.test.ts` → *"claims no deletion timeline this build
does not run"* forbids `deleted on time` and `deleted on schedule`, requires
`stays due` / `next time your deployment runs the purge job` / `due is not yet
deleted` / `neither brings that forward nor holds it back`, and separately
forbids any timeline phrase in `LAPSED_STAFF_BODY` and `UNVERIFIED_STAFF_BODY`.
It fails on the pre-correction file and passes after.

Two false positives shaped the assertion, both familiar:

- The JSX wraps mid-sentence, so a phrase check has to normalise whitespace
  before matching.
- Unanchored `on schedule` matches inside *"retenti-on schedule"* — the phrase
  these notices are supposed to contain. Word boundaries, not substrings.

Re-verified: `typecheck ✓`, `lint 0 errors / 2 pre-existing warnings`,
**678 tests passed**, `build ✓`. Program & plan checked at 1280×800 and
768×1024: `deleted on time` absent, all four required phrases present, no
`Invalid Date`/`NaN`, no horizontal overflow.

## Deliberately left

- **The gap itself.** A purge can delete a cohort while the term is paused.
  That is arguably correct — retention is the school's policy and the invoice
  should not suspend it, which is the position the Program note now states out
  loud. This sprint made the copy match the behaviour; it did not change the
  behaviour, and a decision to gate the purge on the term would be a product
  change, not a copy correction.
- **No billing or account-management surface** was added, per the review's
  explicit boundary.
