# Sprint 10 review — 26 August 2026

**Scope.** Two defects independent review found in Sprint 09, plus the stale
handoff file. Both findings were correct and both are now fixed and asserted.

**Method.** Ran against the checklist in `../classroom-review.md`. The ordering
defect was reproduced before it was fixed. The evidence rule is verified by
walking every one of the 27 missions through the real repository function, not
by inspection. Reseeded and compared distributions before and after.

---

## Finding 1 — the evidence fix did not fix evidence

Sprint 09 gave every new decision scene two or more non-looping exits and
claimed that settled it. It did not. `recordDecision` wrote whatever result the
authored choice carried, so a child could take the wrong turn, read the
explanation that tells them the answer, pick the safe option, and be recorded
as having demonstrated the skill independently. `simulateAttempt` did the same,
so the seeded demo carried the same inflation.

**Coaching now downgrades.** A `demonstrated` result recorded on a scene the
child has already answered becomes `developing`, because the correction is what
got them there. `demonstrated` remains sticky once earned unaided anywhere, so
the record still reports what a child can do rather than averaging their
attempts. Both the live path and the seed apply the identical rule.

Measured after reseeding: primary-skill demonstration among completers moved
off 100% into the mid-90s, and `developing` now appears in all nine skills
where before it appeared in almost none.

### The regression

`tests/evidence-integrity.test.ts` walks a **retry-first path through all 27
missions** using the real `recordDecision`, then rebuilds from the stored path
alone which skills could honestly have been earned — a strong choice on a scene
not already answered — and asserts nothing outside that set is reported as
demonstrated. A first-try walk is asserted to still demonstrate, so the rule
cannot be satisfied by simply recording less. Stickiness has its own test.

Sixty assertions, one pair per mission plus the curriculum-wide rules.

## Finding 2 — skill triplets were blocked practice

Also correct, and worse than reported. Orders ran 1–3 privacy.identity, 4–6
privacy.media, and so on, which meant:

| Assigned | Competencies | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| first 9 | **1 of 3 — privacy only** | 3 of 9 |
| first 15 | 2 of 3 | 5 of 9 |
| first 18 | 2 of 3 | 6 of 9 |

The seeded classes carry 18, 15, 11 and 9 assignments, so every demo class was
skewed this way too. The Sprint 09 review's claim that a teacher assigning in
order "gets spaced practice for free" was simply false, and is now annotated as
such in that file rather than quietly edited.

**Three interleaved passes of nine.** Each pass touches all nine skills once,
rotating through the competencies, so the three encounters with a skill sit
exactly nine missions apart. Every prefix is balanced:

| Assigned | Competencies | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| first 3 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 9 |
| first 9 | 3 of 3 | **9 of 9** |
| any prefix | 3 of 3 | — |

Asserted directly in the new suite, so adding missions cannot silently
reintroduce blocking.

## Reporting language

Updated everywhere the old rule was described: the teacher class page
(competency evidence and the roster legend), the teacher overview, and the
school report. Each now says that a skill counts when chosen **unaided**, and
that reaching the same answer after a Try again records as developing.

The student view needed more than a wording change. A child who needed the
explanation would simply have stopped seeing that skill listed, which reads as
having lost something. There are now two lists: *Things you have shown you can
do* — got on your own, first go — and *Things you are getting the hang of*, in
dashed outline, with the line "That still counts, and they move up when you get
one first go." The teacher-facing signal stays precise; the child-facing one
stays encouraging.

## Deliberately left

- **The eight legacy forced-award scenes.** Sprint 09's missions all have two
  or more non-looping exits; the original nine do not. Those scenes now
  interact with the coaching rule in a way nobody has traced, and that trace is
  the right first task for the next integrity pass.
- **Stickiness across three encounters.** One unaided success out of three is
  enough to hold `demonstrated`. That is the specified rule and it keeps
  aggregate rates high; whether a teacher reads 95% correctly is a question for
  a real teacher, not for a test.
- **Interleaving assumes sequential assignment.** A teacher who assigns by
  competency or cherry-picks gets none of the benefit. Worth revisiting if the
  mission library grows a "suggested next" control.
